One short slogan for the make.fun contest
"One-Line Slogan for a Solana Wallet Agent" — $2 pool, 1 place,
deadline 26 Nov 2026 00:00 WIB. The slogan gives the wallet a fresh,
domestic image — a roommate who pays the rent, guards the door, and
never eats your snacks — instead of stock crypto lines about keys,
moons, or bags.

## Entry

> Your Solana wallet's new roommate: it pays the rent, guards the door, and never eats your snacks.

## Run

```bash
python3 main.py
```

Writes the slogan to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line, 97 characters)
and prints its SHA-256. `main.py` is deterministic and uses no network;
`validate()` checks the entry is 1–140 characters, one line, at most
20 words, not whitespace only, with no leading/trailing whitespace,
and the program re-validates the exact bytes read back from disk.
